description: User's visual constraints for Waste2Worth AI home-page revisions.

Use the user's real agricultural photos on the home page, not generated images, stock photos, or cartoon landscape illustrations.

**Why:** The user wants a professional, welcoming home page connected to real agricultural work and rejected the decorative sun, hills, and plant as childish.

**How to apply:** Keep the farmer photo prominent and rice husk/straw photos as supporting context. Use descriptive alt text and suitable crops without distortion or heavy filters. If an uploaded photo is unavailable, show a neutral area and tell the user which photo is missing; do not substitute unrelated imagery.
